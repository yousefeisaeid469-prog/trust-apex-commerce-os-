# TRUST V124 — Prevention OS

V124 adds a preventive layer above Problem Solver. Instead of waiting for a delivery failure, stockout, return, price surprise, seller concern, payment friction, fit issue, or abandoned cart, the platform can evaluate a signal and select a guarded intervention.

## Principles
- Evidence before intervention.
- Reversible interventions first.
- No fabricated logistics, inventory, pricing, or payment state.
- No dark patterns or fake scarcity.
- Material trust decisions require human review.

## Production boundary
The engine is deterministic decision-support scaffolding. Live carrier, inventory, payment, seller-risk, pricing-history, and analytics providers are not connected by this release.
