# TRUST V127 — Consumer Rights + Financial Intelligence

V127 expands TRUST from protection checks into two durable decision layers:

1. Consumer Rights OS — evidence-first checks for price, delivery, authenticity, returns, warranty, payment and seller disclosure.
2. Financial Intelligence — total-cost and affordability analysis across upfront price, shipping, fees, expected return cost, maintenance and expected lifespan.

## Integrity rules
- Unknown provider data remains unknown; it is never silently converted into a positive claim.
- Estimates are explicitly labeled as estimates.
- High-impact decisions remain reviewable.
- No fabricated savings, guarantees, delivery promises or authenticity claims.

## Production boundary
The engines are deterministic domain foundations. Production-grade results require connected payment, carrier, catalog, warranty, seller-verification and pricing providers plus durable event processing and observability.
