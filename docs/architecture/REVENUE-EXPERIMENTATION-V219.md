# TRUST APEX OS — V219 Revenue Experimentation & Learning

V219 closes the analytical loop after V218 by evaluating controlled revenue experiments using integer minor-unit economics and explicit evidence gates.

## Core
- Control/treatment conversion and revenue lift.
- Incremental contribution modeling after measured costs.
- Evidence score and balance guardrails.
- Decisions: SCALE, CONTINUE, REVERT, INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE.
- Learning plan produces next actions without changing live economics.

## Boundaries
- No automatic price, ad, payment, credit, or provider changes.
- Results are evidence/modeling outputs, not guaranteed future revenue.
- Causal claims require adequate, balanced measurement.
