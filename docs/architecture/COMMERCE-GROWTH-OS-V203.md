# TRUST V203 — Commerce Growth OS

V203 turns the earlier Amazon-parity capability map into an executable, provider-neutral growth layer. It focuses on the mechanisms that create durable marketplace economics: transparent offer ranking, controlled deals, recurring subscriptions, reorder intelligence, referral economics, advertising eligibility, B2B services, fulfillment services and auditable revenue streams.

## Principles
- No hidden seller favoritism: Buy Box-style ranking is explainable and stock-aware.
- No fake revenue: revenue events are minor-unit ledger records with evidence-bearing source IDs.
- No automatic financial side effects from recommendation code.
- Ads require disclosure, frequency controls and policy gates.
- Subscriptions expose renewal/cancellation boundaries.
- Provider integrations remain adapters, not hard-coded vendor dependencies.

## Amazon-pattern parity translated into TRUST
Amazon publicly describes revenue categories including third-party seller services, advertising and subscription services, while seller programs include fulfillment, multichannel fulfillment, global selling, warehousing, advertising, subscriptions and seller AI tooling. TRUST models these as independent capability and revenue surfaces rather than copying proprietary implementation.
