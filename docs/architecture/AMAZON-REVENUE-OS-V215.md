# TRUST APEX OS V215 — Amazon Revenue OS

V215 maps the major Amazon-inspired commerce monetization engines into one auditable, provider-neutral layer.

## Revenue surfaces
- First-party retail / product margin
- Third-party seller services / marketplace commission
- Advertising
- Subscriptions and memberships
- Fulfillment
- Seller services and brand services
- Affiliate commerce
- B2B procurement and business membership
- Supply-chain services
- Financial-services infrastructure
- Devices and media
- Other commerce services

## Guardrails
Recommendations are not revenue. Revenue requires evidence from an order, provider, ledger or other verifiable source. No provider credentials, credit decision, payment capture, or automatic financial side effect is created by V215.

## Amazon alignment
Amazon's public materials describe major revenue categories including product sales, third-party seller services, advertising services, subscription services and other services. Amazon also publicly operates programs spanning FBA/MCF, Global Selling, Amazon Business, advertising, Subscribe & Save and supply-chain services. V215 models the capability architecture without claiming live Amazon integration.
