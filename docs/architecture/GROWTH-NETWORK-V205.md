# Growth Network V205

V205 turns existing merchant orders and catalog records into a provider-neutral growth network. It deliberately uses observed customer IDs and product co-occurrence rather than inferred identity attributes or fabricated behavior.

## Loops
- Reorder: repeated customers become candidates for a measured repeat-purchase flow.
- Cross-sell: co-purchased products form directional recommendation evidence.
- Loyalty: repeat-rate can justify a controlled retention experiment.
- Referral: a completed customer base can support referral testing.
- Demand capture: observed demand can prioritize discovery and sponsored-placement experiments.
- Seller promotion: controlled deals/coupons can be proposed without automatic financial execution.

## Truth boundary
Revenue and AOV are calculated from available order totals only. Margin, payout, CAC, provider spend and campaign performance are not invented. External messaging, ads, subscriptions and financial actions require explicit adapters, consent and policy gates.
