# TRUST V270.0.0 — Marketplace Growth & Monetization Runtime

V270 breaks the previous audit-only loop by shipping production-oriented commerce surfaces backed by PostgreSQL:

- seller plans and marketplace fee ledger
- sponsored product/brand/display campaigns with daily-budget enforcement and idempotent click charges
- customer membership and product-subscription persistence
- fulfillment program economics
- B2B account and quantity-price persistence
- affiliate attribution/commission ledger

This release does not claim full Amazon parity. Amazon exposes many additional programs and services; V270 implements a concrete revenue-critical subset rather than generating another evidence-only layer.
