# TRUST V126 — Protection OS

TRUST V126 adds an evidence-first purchase protection layer above Prevention, Life OS and Problem Solver.

## Protection domains
- Counterfeit risk
- Scam-pattern review
- Price fairness
- Seller integrity
- Warranty gaps
- Return-clause clarity
- Payment safety
- Listing/product mismatch

## Safety model
The engine is deterministic decision support. It does not prove fraud/counterfeiting from a single signal, does not fabricate price history, and does not execute material financial actions by itself. High-impact cases can be routed to human review.

## Production boundary
Connect trusted authenticity/catalog data, fraud/seller reputation, price history, warranty/returns, payment status and order evidence providers before making production enforcement decisions.
