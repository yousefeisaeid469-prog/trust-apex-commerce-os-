# V63 Architecture
Browser Monetization: contextual ads, sponsorships, trend subscriptions and rewards with consent/frequency caps.
Supply Chain: demand forecast → restock → RFQ draft → supplier ranking → approval.
Finance: transparent merchant-health metrics and lender-ready summaries; no direct lending decision.
Audit: chained SHA-256 hashes provide tamper evidence without claiming blockchain.
Fraud: velocity, return, payment and privacy-minimized risk signals produce risk bands and verification recommendations.
Personalization: Fit Twin, Arabic Voice Commerce and AI Stylist share consent-aware preferences.
Social: shared carts and style battles with explicit ownership and payment boundaries.
Inventory Radar: merchant-approved availability with privacy-minimized geographic display.
