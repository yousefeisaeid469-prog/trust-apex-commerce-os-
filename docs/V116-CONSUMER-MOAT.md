# V116 Consumer Moat

The goal is not to copy Amazon, Temu or SHEIN feature-for-feature. TRUST should create a differentiated reason to stay.

## Moats
1. **Trust Layer** — verified sellers, provenance evidence, review-quality signals and transparent policies.
2. **Price Confidence** — price-watch and eligible price-adjustment workflows; no fake guarantees.
3. **Delivery Confidence** — promised ETA, milestone tracking and proactive exception communication.
4. **Post-Purchase OS** — returns, exchanges, refunds and support are first-class experiences.
5. **Personal Discovery** — session-aware recommendations with clear reasons instead of opaque manipulation.
6. **TRUST Circle** — loyalty, early access and perks through a durable ledger.
7. **Concierge** — AI-first assistance with human escalation for complex cases.
8. **Seller Quality** — merchant quality scoring and marketplace governance to keep supply trustworthy.
9. **Local-first advantage** — support local payment, delivery and language experiences while keeping the platform globally extensible.
10. **Community & content** — creator collections, authentic reviews and useful shopping guides that compound discovery.

## North Star
Make customers feel that TRUST is the safest, clearest and easiest place to buy — not merely the place with the lowest sticker price.
