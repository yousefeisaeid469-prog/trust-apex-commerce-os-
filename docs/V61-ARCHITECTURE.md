# V61 Architecture

## Intelligence Graph
Connects merchants, products, customers, inventory, orders, campaigns and
operational events through governed relationships.

## Network Effects
Aggregated, privacy-minimized signals improve benchmarks, demand matching,
inventory discovery and marketplace recommendations without exposing raw
customer data unnecessarily.

## Customer Journey Orchestrator
Coordinates consent-aware lifecycle states: discover, consider, purchase,
receive, return, repeat and win-back.

## AI Quality Center
Tracks accuracy, drift, latency, cost, tool failures, unsafe-action rate and
business impact, with release gates.

## Incident Copilot
Correlates anomalies, recent changes, service health and business metrics,
then proposes reversible remediation.

## Governance
Capabilities use scoped permissions, policy checks, rate limits, confidence
thresholds, audit logs and approval gates where appropriate.
