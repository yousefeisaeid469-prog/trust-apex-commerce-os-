# TRUST V60 Architecture

## Decision Loop
Signal → Context → Goal → Plan → Simulate → Policy → Approval → Execute →
Verify → Measure → Learn.

## Commerce Autopilot
The autopilot does not receive unrestricted authority. It operates through
scoped capabilities and the Safe Action Gateway.

## Opportunity Radar
Ranks opportunities using expected value, confidence, urgency, reversibility,
margin impact and operational risk.

## Unit Economics
Evaluates revenue, COGS, fulfillment, payment fees, returns and acquisition
cost before recommending actions.

## Campaign Studio
Generates campaign plans, forecasts impact, simulates scenarios and prepares
execution packages.

## Retention Engine
Coordinates lifecycle interventions using consent, frequency caps and
measurable holdouts.

## AI Boardroom
Executive-level summaries of business health, opportunities, risks,
experiments, incidents and recommended decisions.

## Decision Ledger
Every consequential AI recommendation gets an auditable rationale, policy
result, actor, timestamp, inputs and outcome.
