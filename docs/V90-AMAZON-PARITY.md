# TRUST V90 — Amazon-Parity Capability Expansion

V90 adds a capability map and domain contracts for major marketplace capabilities: offer ranking/Buy Box-style selection, subscriptions, deals/coupons, gifting, price alerts, Q&A, personalization, reordering, membership, fulfillment promises, multi-warehouse operations, seller health, automated pricing, advertising, brand stores/rich content, B2B pricing and approval, purchase orders, global selling, returns/refunds/claims, customer support, product comparison, forecasting, restock, review moderation, brand protection, referrals, analytics, experimentation and omnichannel inventory.

The contracts are deliberately provider-neutral. No Amazon trademarks, proprietary APIs, or Amazon credentials are embedded.
