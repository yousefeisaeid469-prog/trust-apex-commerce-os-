# TRUST V125 — Autonomous Life OS

V125 adds a privacy-first personal commerce layer on top of Prevention OS.

## Capabilities
- Commerce Memory: explicit, consented preference/purchase signals.
- Warranty Intelligence: surface warranty/service checks before unnecessary replacement.
- Refill Planning: suggest replenishment from purchase cadence without auto-ordering.
- Family Shopping: prepare choices and require approval for shared/family missions.
- Deadline Buying: prioritize options around a user-provided deadline without inventing delivery promises.
- Preference Matching: explain how saved preferences affect recommendations.

## Safety / trust boundary
No autonomous purchase is performed. Material actions are confirmation or human-review gated. Sensitive data should not be stored unless required and consented. Live warranty, identity, subscription, inventory, delivery and payment providers remain integration boundaries.
